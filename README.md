# ImbaLink 🏠

### Find a place. Connect with people. Build better.

**ImbaLink** is a modern property discovery and connection platform built to make finding rooms, houses, and accommodation simpler, more transparent, and more connected.

Instead of relying on scattered listings and difficult-to-navigate social media groups, ImbaLink brings **property seekers, landlords, and contractors together in one digital ecosystem.**

## 🚀 Our Vision

Finding a home should be simple.

ImbaLink is being built with a bigger vision: to create a trusted platform where people can **discover properties, connect directly with owners, request viewings, communicate securely, and ultimately turn a place into a home.**

> **We're not just building a property platform. We're building a better way to connect people with places.**

## ✨ What ImbaLink Offers

* 🏠 **Property Discovery** — Find rooms, houses, cottages, and other accommodation.
* 🔎 **Smart Search** — Discover properties based on location and preferences.
* 💬 **Messaging** — Connect directly with landlords and other users.
* 📅 **Viewing Requests** — Request, accept, or decline property viewings.
* 👤 **User Profiles** — Build trusted profiles and showcase relevant information.
* 🤝 **Student Connections** — Discover compatible students and potential roommates.
* 🛠️ **Contractors** — Connect property owners and tenants with service providers.
* 🔐 **Verification** — Support for identity verification and trusted interactions.
* 📍 **Location-Based Discovery** — Make finding nearby opportunities easier.
* ❤️ **Saved Properties** — Keep track of properties you're interested in.

## 🇿🇼 Built for Zimbabwe, Built to Grow

ImbaLink starts with Zimbabwe at its heart, addressing real challenges people face when searching for accommodation and connecting with property owners.

The goal is bigger than one city or one country.

**ImbaLink is designed to grow into a platform where technology makes finding a place to live faster, easier, and more human.**
